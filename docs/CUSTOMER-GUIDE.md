# Smart Retail POS: getting started

For the person who runs the business. No technical words. If anything here does not match what you see, call **smartave99@gmail.com** or **+91 6123115368**, or the company you bought it from.

## 1. Install

Run **SmartRetailPOS-Hub-Setup-…exe** on the PC that stays at your counter (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit). Click Next. If Windows says *unknown publisher*, choose *More info → Run anyway*. When it finishes, an icon **Smart Retail POS** is on your desktop. It runs quietly in the background and starts by itself when the PC starts.

## 2. Activate

Click the icon. A window of its own opens (no address bar, nothing to type but your key) and says *This program needs a licence*. Type your **licence key** (it looks like `NGOS-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`) and press **Activate**. You need the internet for this one time. No internet at the counter? Tell your supplier: they give you a code to type instead.

## 3. Set up your business

A short form asks for: the name of your business, your **country** (it decides your money, your tax and the words on your bills), the **kind of business** (shop, restaurant, library, builder, salon or services, wholesale, or any other), and your own name and password. Tick **Fill with a sample business** if you want to look around first (it cannot be taken out later, so do not tick it on your real PC).

## 4. Your first day

| If you run ... | Start here |
|---|---|
| a **shop** | *Products*: add what you sell (name, price, tax). *New sale*: scan a barcode or type a name, take payment, **Complete**. |
| a **restaurant or café** | *Tables*, then take an order; the *Kitchen* screen shows what to cook; bill the table (tips, service charge, split). |
| a **library** | *People* (members) and *Titles* with their copies; then the *Desk*: lend, return and renew; fines are worked out for you. |
| a **builder** | *Projects*: the bill of quantities, then progress bills with retention; costs and changes. |
| a **salon or clinic** | *Bookings* by person; when they come, charge the visit with any products and a tip. |
| a **wholesaler** | Add customers with a credit limit; sell on credit; take part payments; see what is owed. |

Each screen explains itself in plain words. In **Settings** you can change your business details, tax rates (ask your tax adviser to check them), the words the screens use, who can sign in and what each person may do, printers, and the **Look** (colours and logo, if your licence allows).

## 5. Printers and scanners

A scanner that plugs in (USB) or pairs by Bluetooth works like a keyboard: click the box and scan. A phone or tablet camera can scan too. For printers go to **Settings → Printers**, choose how it is connected (network, USB/system printer, or Bluetooth/serial port), and press **Test page**. Price tags and posters print from your browser to any printer.

## 6. Keep your data safe

Your information is in one folder on this PC, `C:\ProgramData\NextGenOS\Hub`. **Copy that folder to a USB drive or cloud drive regularly** (close the program first: Windows *Services* → *Smart Retail POS Hub* → Stop). Uninstalling the program does not delete it.

## 7. Help

Settings → Licence shows your licence and who to call. For a problem, tell us: what you pressed, what you expected, what happened, and the time.
